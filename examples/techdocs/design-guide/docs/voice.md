# Voice

Words in the portal exist to make it easier to use. They are design material.

- **Name things by what people control.** "Owner", not "spec.owner".
- **Say what happens.** Buttons name the action: *Register component*, not
  *Submit*. The toast that follows uses the same verb.
- **Errors explain and point.** What went wrong, and the one thing to try.
  No apologies.
- **Sentence case, plain verbs, no filler.** "Search services, APIs, docs and
  teams" — not "Discover your engineering ecosystem".
- **The product is Accelerate; the identity is the customer's.** "Sign in with
  CustomerX SSO to open Accelerate" — where `CustomerX` is a placeholder for
  the customer's name in all user-facing copy until the real brand lands.
